Built Around Simplicity and Transparency.

Friend Pocket aims to provide a simple digital experience for people exploring financial support options.

Friend Pocket was created to make exploring financial support options less confusing — clear information, an honest process, and a simple way to check where you stand before you commit to anything.

We believe the application experience itself shouldn't add stress to what can already be a difficult moment. That's the idea behind every screen in this product.
